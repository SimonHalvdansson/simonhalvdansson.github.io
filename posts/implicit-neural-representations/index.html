Presentation on INRs

By Simon Halvdansson | Nov. 2026

I recently put together a short presentation about implicit neural representations at work using Opus 5.5 which I thought was nice and wanted to put up online as well.

Figure: Screenshot from the presentation, press the image to open it.

It's nothing novel or particularly innovative, but it does capture how I think about the basics of this problem and I think it does a good job of explaining things. INRs are particularly well suited for these types of visual explanations which is why I was inclined to try out one of these LLM-HTML presentations which you see frequently nowadays. To be clear, to produce this I wrote a rought script (~750 words) which I handed to Opus 5.5 and gave a few rounds of feedback with changes to all of the slides but nothing too major. This is a nice way to produce a presentation of customized presentation of a somewhat-well-known topic without spending too much time. Hopefully
